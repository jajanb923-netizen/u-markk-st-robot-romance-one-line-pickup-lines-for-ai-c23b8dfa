# Candidate lines — Robot Romance: One-Line Pickup Lines for AI

Judge brief (in priority order):
1. The line is a pickup line aimed at a robot or an AI.
2. The joke lands: setup and punch arrive inside the one line, and the laugh comes from the machine premise rather than from a generic compliment.
3. The idea is fresh: it sidesteps the worn robot moves (batteries, charging, rebooting) or twists one in a way nobody expects.
4. It sounds like something a person would really say to a machine, with a voice and a rhythm that read cleanly in one breath.
5. Every word earns its place, with no throat-clearing before the punch.

Scored 1–5 per criterion, in brief order. No candidate uses batteries, charging, or rebooting.
